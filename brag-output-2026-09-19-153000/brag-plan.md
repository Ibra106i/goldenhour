# Brag Plan: Golden Hour Coffee Co.

## What is this app?
A cozy neighborhood café website for Golden Hour Coffee Co. in East Austin — warm, sun-drenched, designed to make you want to sit down with a latte and stay all morning.

## The angle
This isn't a tech product launch. This is a love letter to slow mornings. The video should feel like the first sip of a Golden Hour Latte — warm, inviting, and exactly what you needed. The hook is the vibe itself: that golden light through the front window, the smell of espresso, the feeling of nowhere else to be.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The sunlit café interior fills the screen — warm light, trailing ivy, rustic wood. A single word fades in: **"linger."** That's the whole pitch in one word.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The terracotta hero card slides up with "Slow mornings, strong coffee." — the tagline lands like a promise
- Featured dishes appear one by one: Golden Hour Latte, Whipped Ricotta Toast, Cinnamon Roll Skillet — each with its price tag and "Best Seller" / "Chef's Pick" badge
- The testimonials section: "Loved by Locals" with three 5-star reviews sliding in

## Outro / punchline
The Golden Hour logo (the "G" in the terracotta circle) holds center frame. Below it: **"Open Daily 7AM–3PM · East Austin, TX"** — quiet confidence. No call to action needed. The vibe sells itself.

## User flow worth showing
none — landing-page only. This is a multi-page marketing SPA with no working app flow. The video recreates the strongest landing page sections instead.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: "quiet premium neighborhood café film — warm, inviting, sun-drenched"
- Interpretation: Fewer scenes, longer holds. Confidence through restraint. The product speaks for itself through warm imagery and clean typography. No rush.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #FBF3E7 (cream)
- Accent: #C1633D (terracotta)
- Text: #3B2A20 (espresso)
- Secondary: #8A9A5B (sage), #E08E45 (burnt-orange)
- Display font: Kalam (cursive script)
- Body font: Inter (sans-serif)
- Strongest visual element: the terracotta hero card with rounded corners overlapping the sunlit café photo

## Share copy (draft)
Golden Hour Coffee Co. — where slow mornings meet strong coffee. ☀️

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed — gentle acoustic guitar, ambient café texture
- Music: warm acoustic guitar track, low and inviting
- Music treatment: fade in at 0s, stay low under visuals, gentle swell at the logo reveal, fade out at 20s
- Music cue guidance: cues to be detected at composition time; target the dish reveals and testimonial arrivals for beat-aligned moments
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — music RMS could gently warm the cream background tint, nothing aggressive
- SFX posture: sparse — one soft "cup set down" sound at the hook, gentle card滑动 sounds for dish reveals, star rating shimmer
- Audio-coupled moments: dish cards arrive one by one with subtle beat-aligned timing; testimonials slide in on gentle accents
- Restraint rule: no energetic beats, no whooshes, no startup energy. This is a café, not a product launch.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Golden Light — 5s
Full-bleed sunlit café interior photo (the arch-framed image from the hero). Warm light fills the screen. The word **"linger."** fades in at center, Kalam script, cream-colored, with a soft drop shadow. Holds for 3 seconds.
Sequential/interaction: none — single image, single word
Audio intent: warm acoustic guitar fades in, ambient café texture underneath
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: warm, low, inviting
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Promise — 5s
The terracotta hero card slides up from below, overlapping the café photo slightly. The headline **"Slow mornings, strong coffee."** animates in with a gentle fade. Below it, the tagline body text appears: "A cozy neighborhood café where locals linger over pour-overs…" The "Reserve a Table" and "View Menu" buttons appear with a subtle bounce.
Sequential/interaction: card slides up → headline fades in → body text fades in → buttons appear
Audio intent: music continues, soft and warm
Audio-coupled idea: dish card slide-in timed to a gentle beat
Music: continuous warm bed
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — From the Kitchen — 6s
Three featured dish cards appear one by one, left to right, each with a slight scale-up entrance:
1. Golden Hour Latte — $5.50 — "Best Seller" badge
2. Whipped Ricotta Toast — $11.00 — "Vegetarian" badge
3. Cinnamon Roll Skillet — $9.00 — "Chef's Pick" badge
Each card holds long enough to read the name and price (~1.5s per card settled).
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 cards arrive one by one, each with a gentle beat-aligned pop
Audio intent: subtle card pop sounds, music stays warm
Audio-coupled idea: each dish card arrival aligned to a beat or gentle accent
Music: continuous, slight warmth increase
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Loved by Locals — 4s
The "Loved by Locals" heading appears. Three testimonial cards slide in from the right, stacked vertically with slight offset:
- Sarah M. — 5 stars — "The Golden Hour Latte with oat milk is pure heaven."
- Marcus K. — 5 stars — "Hands down the best whipped ricotta toast I have ever had!"
- Elena R. — 5 stars — "The Cinnamon Roll Skillet is a must-try."
Each card holds briefly. Star ratings shimmer.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 testimonials arrive one by one with star shimmer
Audio intent: gentle shimmer sound on stars, music stays low
Audio-coupled idea: star shimmer on each testimonial arrival
Music: continuous warm bed
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — The Logo — 5s
Clean cream background. The Golden Hour logo (terracotta circle with "G" in Kalam script) scales in from 0.95 to 1.0 with a soft ease. Below it:
**Golden Hour Coffee Co.**
Open Daily 7AM–3PM · East Austin, TX
The logo holds. Music fades out gently over the last 2 seconds.
Sequential/interaction: logo scales in → text fades in below
Audio intent: music swell at logo arrival, then gentle fade to silence
Audio-coupled idea: none — clean, confident hold
Music: swell then fade out
Transition mood: fade to end

**Music mood for this video:** warm, cinematic, inviting — acoustic guitar bed with ambient texture
**Audio summary:** A warm acoustic guitar fades in over a sunlit café, stays low through dish reveals and testimonials, swells gently at the logo, then fades to silence.
